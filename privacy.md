# Privacy Policy for Stardrops

**Last updated: 24 August 2026**

Stardrops does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party services of any kind. The
developer receives nothing about you or your device.

## What the app stores on your device

Stardrops saves a few settings locally so the game remembers them between
sessions. This information stays on your phone, is never transmitted to the
developer, and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

- The display name you choose for yourself
- How many games you have played and won, per difficulty level
- Your language preference
- Whether sound is on or off
- Whether you have seen the how-to-play screens

## Local multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects phones directly to each other over your own WiFi
network. When you join a game, the display name you chose is sent to the other
players in that game so they can see who is at the table, and their names are
sent to you. Nothing else about you or your device is shared.

These connections do not pass through any server. No game data reaches the
developer, and none of it leaves your local network. The connection exists
only while the game is running.

Your device may ask for permission to find devices on your local network the
first time you host or join a game. That permission is used only to reach the
other players' phones, and the game works fully without it in solo mode.

## Children

Stardrops is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all,
it collects no data from children.
